Privacy Policy for Score Tracker

Effective 17 August 2026 · Last updated 17 August 2026

Score Tracker does not collect any of your data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. It does not request permission to access the internet, and it cannot send information anywhere. Everything you enter stays on your phone.

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Score Tracker Android application (com.cudafishgames.scoretracker), distributed through Google Play by CudaFishGames ("we", "us").

2. Information we collect

We collect nothing. Specifically, Score Tracker does not collect, transmit, sell, or share:

  • Personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number
  • Account credentials — the app has no accounts and no sign-in
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, files, or any other content on your device
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage analytics
  • Crash reports or diagnostic telemetry

Score Tracker contains no advertising, no analytics software, and no third-party tracking components of any kind.

3. Information stored on your device

To work as a score tracker, the app saves the following in its own private storage on your phone:

  • Games you have chosen to save, including the player names and scores you entered
  • Custom game presets you create, including the names you give them
  • App settings, such as your appearance preference and whether the screen stays awake

This information is written only to the app's private storage area on your device. It is never transmitted off your phone, and we have no access to it. We cannot see it, retrieve it, or recover it for you.

The app also offers a Quick score mode, which keeps nothing at all — that data exists only while the game is open.

4. No network access

Score Tracker does not request the Android internet permission. The operating system therefore prevents the app from making any network connection whatsoever. This is not merely a promise about our intentions — it is enforced by Android itself and can be verified in the app's manifest.

5. Backups

The app disables Android's automatic backup feature. Your saved games and presets are not copied to your Google account or included in device backups. They remain solely on the device where you created them.

One consequence worth knowing: if you uninstall the app, switch phones, or reset your device, your saved games will not carry over and cannot be restored.

6. Permissions

Score Tracker requests no Android permissions. It additionally blocks several permissions that would otherwise be included automatically, including internet access, external storage access, and the ability to draw over other apps.

7. Children's privacy

Score Tracker collects no information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because no data is gathered or transmitted, the app cannot knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from a child.

8. Deleting your data

You are in full control of everything the app stores:

  • Delete individual saved games or custom presets from within the app at any time
  • Clear the app's storage through Android's Settings → Apps → Score Tracker → Storage
  • Uninstall the app, which permanently removes all of its data from your device

Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side and no deletion request to submit.

9. Purchases and Google Play

Score Tracker is a paid application. Your purchase is processed entirely by Google Play — we never receive, see, or store your payment details, billing address, or any part of your payment method. Google's handling of that transaction is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play also provides us with aggregate, anonymised statistics about installs and sales as part of its developer tools. This information comes from Google, not from the app, and does not identify individual users.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. If a future version of the app ever collects data — for example, to add an optional feature that shares content between devices — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the corresponding Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated with it.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy or about privacy in Score Tracker can be sent to cudafish.dev@gmail.com.
