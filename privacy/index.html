Privacy Policy for Score Tracker

Effective 17 August 2026 · Last updated 27 September 2026

Score Tracker does not collect any of your data. It has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no crash reporting. It does not request permission to access the internet, and it cannot send information anywhere. Everything you enter stays on your phone, apart from Android's own encrypted backup of your phone (see section 6) and anything you choose to share (see section 5).

1. Who this policy covers

This policy applies to the Score Tracker Android application (com.cudafishgames.scoretracker), distributed through Google Play by CudaFishGames ("we", "us").

2. Information we collect

We collect nothing. Specifically, Score Tracker does not collect, transmit, sell, or share:

  • Personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number
  • Account credentials — the app has no accounts and no sign-in
  • Location data
  • Contacts, photos, files, or any other content on your device
  • Device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage analytics
  • Crash reports or diagnostic telemetry

Score Tracker contains no advertising, no analytics software, and no third-party tracking components of any kind.

This section is about what we do. It does not restrict what you may choose to do with your own scores: you can send a result to someone yourself, which is described in section 5.

3. Information stored on your device

To work as a score tracker, the app saves the following in its own private storage on your phone:

  • Games you have chosen to save, including the player names and scores you entered
  • Custom game presets you create, including the names you give them
  • App settings, such as your appearance preference and whether the screen stays awake
  • A background photo, if you choose one: a smaller copy of the single photo you pick, used only to draw behind the app

Choosing a background photo does not give the app access to your photo library. Android's photo picker hands the app only the one photo you select, and the app keeps a copy of that photo alone. The copy is deleted when you tap Remove photo, when you pick a different photo, or when you clear the app's storage.

This information is written only to the app's private storage area on your device, and we have no access to it. We cannot see it, retrieve it, or recover it for you. The app never transmits it off your phone itself. It can leave in only two ways, and the app performs neither: Android's own backup of your phone, described in section 6, and a result you choose to send as text or as a score card through Android's share sheet, described in section 5.

The app also offers a Quick score mode, which keeps nothing at all — that data exists only while the game is open.

4. No network access

Score Tracker does not request the Android internet permission. The operating system therefore prevents the app from making any network connection whatsoever. This is not merely a promise about our intentions — it is enforced by Android itself and can be verified in the app's manifest.

5. Sharing a result

Score Tracker can share a game's scores two ways, both through Android's standard share sheet: as a plain-text message, and as a score card — an image of the result that the app draws on your phone. These are the only ways anything you have entered can leave the device, and either happens only when you tap "Share text" or "Share card" and pick somewhere to send it.

Three things worth being clear about:

  • Both contain the player names and scores you typed. Those may be other people's names. A score card is a picture, and pictures tend to travel further than messages, so share one the way you would share any photo of the people you played with.
  • The card is drawn on your device. Nothing is uploaded to build it, there is no server involved, and the image file is written to the app's own private storage before you hand it on.
  • We are not involved in the transfer. The app hands the text or the image to Android, you pick an app, and that app sends it under its own permissions and its own privacy policy. Score Tracker still has no network access of its own and still cannot send anything anywhere by itself — see section 4.

Nothing is shared automatically, nothing is shared in the background, and we never see the message or the card, or know that you sent one.

6. Backups

If your phone's backup is turned on (on most phones, Settings → Google → Backup), Android includes Score Tracker's saved games, custom games, settings and background photo in it, the same way it backs up your other apps. When you set up a new phone from that backup, or copy your old phone across, they come back with the app.

  • Android makes the backup, not Score Tracker. The app still has no internet permission and sends nothing itself.
  • It goes to your own Google account. We never receive it and cannot see it.
  • On Android 9 and later it is end-to-end encrypted with your phone's screen lock (PIN, pattern or password), so Google cannot read it either. If your phone has no screen lock, the backup is still encrypted, but not with a secret only you hold.
  • Temporary files, such as score card images made for sharing, are not included.

Android does not let you exclude a single app from its backup. If you would rather none of this is backed up, turn off backup for your phone in Android's settings. You can delete an existing backup from Google Drive, under Backups.

7. Permissions

Score Tracker requests no Android permissions. It additionally blocks several permissions that would otherwise be included automatically, including internet access, external storage access, camera, microphone, photo and video library access, and the ability to draw over other apps. Choosing a background photo needs none of these, because Android's photo picker only ever gives the app the one photo you select.

8. Children's privacy

Score Tracker collects no information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because no data is gathered or transmitted, the app cannot knowingly or unknowingly collect personal information from a child.

9. Deleting your data

You are in full control of everything the app stores:

  • Delete individual saved games or custom presets from within the app at any time
  • Remove a background photo with Remove photo in Settings
  • Clear the app's storage through Android's Settings → Apps → Score Tracker → Storage
  • Uninstall the app, which permanently removes all of its data from your device
  • Delete your phone's backup from Google Drive, under Backups, which removes the copy Android keeps in your Google account (see section 6)

Since we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side and no deletion request to submit.

10. Purchases and Google Play

Score Tracker is a paid application. Your purchase is processed entirely by Google Play — we never receive, see, or store your payment details, billing address, or any part of your payment method. Google's handling of that transaction is governed by the Google Privacy Policy.

Google Play also provides us with aggregate, anonymised statistics about installs and sales as part of its developer tools. This information comes from Google, not from the app, and does not identify individual users.

11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the "last updated" date at the top of this page. If a future version of the app ever collects data — for example, to add an optional feature that shares content between devices — this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the corresponding Google Play Data Safety declaration will be updated with it.
