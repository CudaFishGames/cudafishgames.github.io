Score Tracker

One job, done properly

Score Tracker keeps everyone's score while you play and then gets out of the way. No sign-up, no pop-ups, and nothing to load.

Games already set up

Hearts, Spades, Uno, Flip 7, Rummy, Cribbage, Golf, Darts, Mini Golf, and three ways to score dominoes arrive with the right rules. Or set your own.

Rounds and history

End a round and the totals bank into a per-round table, so you can always see how the game got here.

Your house rules

Save target scores, round settings and how the winner is decided as your own game. Create and edit them right from the home screen, then reuse them forever.

Life totals too

Commander, Standard, Brawl and Two-Headed Giant, with commander damage, poison, energy and experience.

Save or don't

Name a game and it saves as you play. Or use Quick score, which keeps nothing at all.

Share the result

Send the final standings as a message, or as a score card image that shows off the winner.

Make it yours

Pick a background colour, or put your own photo behind the scoreboard, dimmed as much or as little as you like.

Who goes first?

Roll a d20 or a d6, flip a coin, or let the app pick who starts, right from the scoreboard.

Built for the table

Keeps the screen awake between turns. Light, dark, or follow your phone.

A look at it

Everything runs on your device.

It collects nothing

Score Tracker does not request permission to access the internet. Android itself stops it connecting anywhere — so it can't send your data off your phone even if it wanted to.

  • No accounts and no sign-in
  • No advertising, no analytics, no crash reporting
  • No advertising ID, no device identifiers
  • Saved games and presets stay in the app's private storage on your device, and in your phone's own encrypted Android backup if that's on, so a new phone gets them back
  • A background photo is a copy of the one picture you choose — the app never gets access to your photo library
